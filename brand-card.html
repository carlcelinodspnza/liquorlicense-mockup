Palette — measured, not picked

Charcoal, oxblood, amber

Every value below came from pixel-frequency analysis of the supplied PDF. The one judgement call is documented: the source used a deep oxblood for eyebrow text at 1.26:1 — a WCAG failure — so the accent role moved to the brand's own photographic amber at 5.55:1.

Raleway carries dense regulatory body copy: a transfer typically takes 60 to 120 days, depending on background checks, application completeness, local approvals and whether any protests are filed.
