Sell a liquor licence

Fill out the form below and a member of our team will contact you

Type of licence

A licence is a business asset, and it sells like one. We connect sellers with buyers who have already been qualified — institutional groups and independent operators both — so that the people making offers are the people able to complete. The sale is secured in escrow, and the brief throughout is the highest achievable market price for the licence rather than the quickest available exit.

Selling a licence

Are you interested in selling your liquor licence?

Our team is here to help! We will use our extensive network to ensure that we find the absolute best value for your licence. In addition to making sure you get the most out of the sale of your licence, we work to screen any interested buyers to determine that they are qualified under the requirements of the licensing authority.
