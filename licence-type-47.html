Type 47 — On-Sale General, Eating Place

Authorises the sale of beer, wine and distilled spirits for on-premises consumption; beer and wine for off-premises consumption. Must maintain bona fide eating place status.

See Type 47 on the board Ask a broker

Read the last clause as a standing obligation, not a box ticked once at issuance. Bona fide eating place status has to be held for as long as the licence is held, and the two conditions that sit under it — the food-to-alcohol sales ratio and the operating hours — stay live with it. It is also the classification an eating place needs before a spirits list can go on the menu at all — a Type 41 does not reach that far, so the two are not interchangeable and the difference is a whole classification wide.

All five classifications are set side by side on the licence types page. Which one a business needs is decided by what is poured, whether it is consumed where it is bought, and what the room is for — not by size.

In practice

What a Type 47 actually covers

  • Full liquor for on-site consumption at a bona fide eating place
  • Carries a food-to-alcohol sales-ratio condition
  • Carries an operating-hours condition
