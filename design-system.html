Design system

Everything below is inherited, not re-decided. The identity was measured off the client's supplied PDF; the 12 globals were locked once at the web card (Direction C, Tungsten Bloom). Every page overlays these through the wow-* classes.

07 — Shared chrome

Injected identically on every page

The header, mobile drawer and footer rendered on this page are byte-identical to the ones on index.html. Nav is NAV-PATTERNS #13 (caret dropdown + exactly one overlay mega-menu); footer is FOOTER-STYLES #22 (closing CTA row, nav columns, legal row); the mobile sticky bar is STICKY-CTA-BAR #2, carrying both the quote and the phone path.

This is a single-page site, so nav targets are in-page anchors — legitimate here and exempted by verify-nav-integrity, which reserves the real-page-nav rule for multi-page sites and separately checks that every fragment resolves.
