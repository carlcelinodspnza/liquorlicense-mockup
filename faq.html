Buying

Buying a licence

The three that come first: how a purchase actually works, which classification the business needs, and what the licence will cost. Each answer here is the short form — where another page carries the long version, the link sits inside the answer.

How do I buy a liquor licence in California?
You typically purchase an existing licence from a current holder, since most types are capped. The sale is negotiated, the licence placed into escrow, and a transfer application submitted to the ABC. Background checks, public notices and local approvals may also apply.
What is the difference between licence types?
Types differ by what alcohol you may sell and how it is consumed. Type 41 covers beer and wine on-site; Type 47 adds full liquor. Off-sale types 20 and 21 cover alcohol sold for consumption elsewhere. The full definition of each type, and the condition attached to it, sits on the licence types page.
How much does a California liquor licence cost?
It varies widely by type and location — a Type 21 can run from $30,000 to over $500,000 in high-demand areas. Escrow fees, transfer fees, legal assistance and local permits are additional.

Transferring and moving

Approvals, timing and relocation

Once a price is agreed the work becomes approvals. Who signs off, how long that runs, whether a licence can follow the business to a new address — and where in California we can do it. These four set the shape of a transfer once the price is settled.

Do I need city approval before transferring a licence?
Yes. Most transfers need local city or county approval, frequently including zoning clearance and sometimes a Conditional Use Permit. Authorities weigh location, community impact and proximity to schools or churches. Where a permit or a planning hearing is needed, that is Conditional Use Permit work.
How long does a transfer take?
Roughly 60 to 120 days. Background checks, application completeness, local government approvals and any filed protests all move the timeline. Where each of those falls in the sequence is set out in the transfer process.
Can I move a licence to a new location?
Yes, within California, with ABC and local authority approval — a premises-to-premises transfer. Zoning requirements, public notice and city or county approval apply, and some areas carry restrictions or quotas.
Do you work statewide in California?
Yes. We work statewide, across all 58 California counties and their major cities. Which markets we handle most often is set out in where we work.

If you are the seller

Selling a licence you already hold

Buyers are not the only side of a transfer. A holder who is exiting, converting or consolidating meets the same regulator, the same escrow and the same clock — read from the other side of the table. What changes is the order the questions arrive in, not the answers to them.

Seller side

Can you help me sell my liquor licence?

Yes. The licence is marketed to qualified buyers, fair market value is determined, and the transaction is managed from there — negotiations, escrow coordination and ABC compliance.

A sale is a transaction to be run, not a listing to be posted. What the brokerage does on the sell side is set out in full on the services page.
