Before you call

Four things worth having in front of you

None of it is required. It is simply what makes a first call a useful one.

The licence type, or the business goal Type 21, 47 or 48 if you already know which one you are after. If you do not, describe the business and a broker will work back to the type with you.
The county, and the site address if there is one A shortlist of addresses is as useful as a single one. If no site has been chosen yet, the county on its own is a fine starting point.
Whether a site is signed Leased and signed, in escrow, or still being looked at. Say which — it is one of the first questions a broker will put to you.
The date you are aiming at An opening night, a lease start, a closing date. Tell us which one is fixed and which one is only hoped for, because they get handled differently.
