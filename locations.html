Fourteen tabs: one statewide, thirteen markets

How to read this Two conventions run through every tab. A market holding no live listing says so in words and routes to a sourcing brief, so no place name on this page can be mistaken for available stock. Five questions

Tell us the shape of it and we will tell you where to look

Where, what kind of business, which classification, what you are doing, and how to reach you. None of the five is long, and none of them is compulsory.

Step 1 of 5

Which market are you working in?

Pre-filled from the tab you were reading. Change it if you are looking somewhere else — and if your county is not on the list, the last option is the honest one to pick.

What kind of business is it?

The eight we work in. What each one means for licensing is set out sector by sector on the homepage.

Which classification are you after?

If you already know, say so. If you do not, pick Not sure and we will read a suggestion off your answer to the last question. What each one authorises is set out on the classifications page.

Pick a business type on the step before this one and we will read the suggestion off it.

Which side of the table are you on?

Each one is a service we run end to end. What that involves is set out on the services page.

Everything below is optional. Send it half-filled if that is all you have — a county and a phone number is enough to start.
