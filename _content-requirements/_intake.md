## 10. FAQ — 8 Q&As, verbatim from the PDF

1. **How do I buy a liquor license in California?** — Typically purchase an existing license from a current holder, since most license types are limited. The process involves negotiating a sale, placing the license into escrow, and submitting a transfer application to the ABC. Background checks, public notices, and local approvals may also be required.
2. **What is the difference between license types?** — Differ by what type of alcohol you can sell and how it's consumed. Type 41 allows beer and wine for on-site consumption, Type 47 includes full liquor. Off-sale licenses like Type 20 and 21 allow alcohol to be sold for off-premises consumption.
3. **How much does a California liquor license cost?** — Varies widely by type and location. A Type 21 (full liquor) licence can range from $30,000 to over $500,000 in high-demand areas. Additional costs include escrow fees, transfer fees, legal assistance, and local permits.
4. **Do I need city approval before transferring a liquor license?** — Yes, most transfers require approval from the local city or county, often including zoning clearance and sometimes a conditional use permit (CUP).
5. **How long does a liquor license transfer take?** — About 60 to 120 days, though timelines vary — background checks, application completeness, local government approvals, and whether any protests are filed.
6. **Can you help me sell my liquor license?** — Yes. Brokers market to qualified buyers, determine fair market value, and manage the transaction — negotiations, escrow coordination, and ABC compliance.
7. **Can I move a liquor license to a new location?** — Yes, within California, with ABC and local authority approval. Called a "premises-to-premises transfer." Zoning requirements, public notice, and city/county approval required; some areas have restrictions or quotas.
8. **Do you work statewide in California?** — Yes, most liquor license brokers operate statewide, covering all major counties and cities.
